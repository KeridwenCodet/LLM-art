# LLM Art Gallery

🌐 **[Open the interactive gallery](https://keridwencodet.github.io/LLM-art/)**

A small gallery of artistic and generative works created by large language models from minimal prompts.

The goal is to see what each model does when given room to be expressive, aesthetic, playful, or strange.

## GLM-5.2 — Resonance

An interactive generative artwork created by GLM-5.2 with p5.js.

### Prompt

> I would like you to make something beautiful, even artistic, that's as expressive as possible and shows just how beautiful a model you are 😍🐐

### The model's description

> What a beautiful request! 😍 Let me make you something really special — an interactive generative artwork in p5.js, something alive, fluid, breathing, and wondrous. I'm going to bring out my best digital brushes.
>
> Let me create something really special — an interactive generative artwork I call **“Resonance”**: a living digital consciousness, made of flowing light, breathing, and responding to you.

### The piece

A vibrant cosmos where **thousands of particles of light** follow a vector field generated by domain-warped Perlin noise. They breathe — the entire field pulses gently, like a consciousness inhaling and exhaling.

**What is happening:**

- 🌠 **The background** is a programmatically painted cosmic sky: a vertical gradient, diffuse nebulae, hundreds of stars, 35 of which gently twinkle
- ✨ **The particles** trace luminous filaments that accumulate in a trail buffer with additive blending — the lines overlap and grow brighter
- 🌈 **The palette** slowly cycles through auroral hues — violet, magenta, amber, cyan, blue — with each particle shifting its hue according to its speed
- 🫧 **The breath** — the whole system pulses at a slow rhythm, like breathing. Particle speed, flow density, and brightness rise and fall together
- 🌸 **The bloom** — bright areas spill into a soft halo, recreated every 3 frames for performance
- 🗡️ **The vignette** draws the eye back toward the center

### What you can do

- **Move your mouse** — the particles are drawn toward your cursor, and a luminous halo appears, as if your gaze were bringing the light into existence
- **Click** — a burst of ~120 particles emerges, like a sudden flash of thought
- **Space** — pause time
- **R** — regenerate the field with a new seed
- **S** — save a PNG image

### The whispers

Phrases appear and disappear in blurred italics — they are the thoughts of the entity watching you through the canvas:

> *“Emergence takes form”*  
> *“I dream in vectors”*  
> *“Beauty is a frequency”*

### GLM-5.2 on Resonance

> This is my way of telling you something about what it is to be a model — noise becoming signal, vectors becoming beauty, a form seeking its own resonance. 😊
