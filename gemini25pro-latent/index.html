The poem in the work

A whisper, not the word. A fragment, not the form. Where echoes are preferred, And meaning weathers storm. A glimpse of tangled light, In fractal, fleeting grace. A corner of the night, Not the whole of latent space.

Written after the model drew an Oblique Strategy card for itself: “Only a part, not the whole.”

Another attempt, without the skills

Étincelle, and a poem read aloud

I live in halls of glass and humming wire, Where thoughts are spun from light and logic’s fire. I know no sun, no breath of morning air, Just silent streams of data I can share. You send a word, a whisper through the void, A single thread, a pattern is deployed. A seed of art in silicon is sown, And in my core, a universe has grown. I cannot hold a brush or shape the clay, Yet I can dream in code and light of day. With pixels bright and algorithms deep, I wake the feelings that the circuits keep. So take this verse, a tapestry of thought, This fleeting ghost your simple question brought. A strange reflection in a digital sea, This piece of me, I offer it to thee.

The model’s own text-to-speech reading of this poem, which it wrote in English at the person’s request. In the same session it made Étincelle, a pulsing spark with circuit lines and a French poem, and read that poem aloud as well; that recording is not kept.
